# LASERSTONE™ — Scientific and Educational Charter

Approved design direction: 2026-11-26.

## الرسالة
نشرح للطالب، ونُفهّم المدرّس، ونمكّن الباحث، ونُعين المحكّم، ونحتكم إلى الدليل، ونلتزم بالصرامة العلمية ودقة النتائج وجمال الإخراج.

## Five pathways
- Student: accessible explanations, symbols, units, worked examples and exercises.
- Educator: derivations, conceptual illustrations, teaching examples and assessment.
- Researcher: reproducible calculations, explicit assumptions, inputs and provenance.
- Reviewer: equation, dimension, unit, reference, methodology and novelty checks.
- Validation: domain checks, uncertainty, benchmarks and experimental evidence where available.

## Shared core and acceptance
All pathways use the same versioned constants, quantities, units, equations and evidence. Presentation may differ, not physical facts. Each applicable module documents its validity domain, sources, uncertainty and evidence status. Test numerical and invalid inputs, dimensional and semantic consistency, and traceability. Distinguish synthetic tests, reference benchmarks, operational acceptance and experimental validation. Preserve bilingual clarity, confidentiality and owner approval for release. Document and justify non-applicable pathways.

This charter establishes design requirements, not proof of implementation or scientific validation.

العلم بالمشاورة، والحكم بالدليل، والتنفيذ بالموافقة، والأسرار في أمانة أصحابها.

From Understanding to Discovery, from Discovery to Verification.

من الفهم إلى الاكتشاف، ومن الاكتشاف إلى التحقق.
