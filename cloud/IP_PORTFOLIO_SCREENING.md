# LASERSTONE™ — Controlled Release and IP Screening Plan
Status: DRAFT; internal screening framework, not a patent filing or legal opinion.

## Approved phase-one scope
- Only Arabic and English are enabled in the public preview.
- Other languages are deferred pending reviewed translations, performance tests and owner approval.
- Public site is informational; private research workspaces and simulation services remain disabled.
- No public deployment until the owner reviews repository visibility, third-party licenses, confidentiality and potential patent disclosures.

## Patent portfolio strategy
Do not assume that one platform equals one patent, or that each Python module qualifies for a separate patent. Map technical contributions into *candidate invention families*, assess independently and consolidate related claims where legally appropriate. Multiple filings may be possible only when each application meets the applicable requirements and is supported by a sufficiently detailed disclosure.

### Internal screening categories — NOT assertions of novelty
1. **Simulation exchange and provenance:** technical data import, integrity checking, interoperability and traceability, where an actual novel technical solution is identified.
2. **Physics consistency and validation:** technical methods for testing physical consistency and computational output, distinct from mathematical rules as such.
3. **Numerical execution and reliability:** any demonstrable technical improvement in solver operation, resource use, reproducibility or fault isolation.
4. **Measurement/simulation integration:** a concrete technical process or apparatus coupling measurements and numerical models, if supported by implementation and evidence.
5. **Controlled research computing:** security, isolation and permission enforcement only if a non-obvious technical advance beyond standard web access control exists.

These categories are exploratory and may yield ZERO patentable inventions. General scientific equations, abstract ideas, routine software features, presentation of information and unsubstantiated benefits should not be treated as patentable inventions by default.

## Candidate assessment (keep confidential)
For each candidate, prepare a **private** invention disclosure containing:
- inventor(s), contribution dates, employment/assignment and collaborator rights;
- technical problem, technical solution, distinguishing features and alternatives;
- architecture, implementation, enabling examples and evidence of technical effect;
- closest prior art, novelty differences and inventive-step rationale;
- public disclosures already made (GitHub history, manuscripts, talks, demos), with exact dates;
- possible overlap with other candidates and unity-of-invention concerns;
- proposed filing sequence and pre-filing confidentiality controls.

Do NOT commit invention disclosures, claims drafts, undisclosed algorithm details or unpublished datasets to this repository unless repository visibility, history and access are verified and authorized.

## Legal verification checklist
Check current Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP) rules, regulations, fee schedules and filing requirements with an appropriately qualified Saudi patent professional before deciding:
- patentable subject matter and treatment of computer-implemented inventions;
- novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability and sufficient disclosure;
- unity of invention, divisional applications, priority, and filing timing;
- effects of previous disclosure and any exceptions;
- ownership, employment agreements, assignment and joint inventorship;
- trademark search and filing for LASERSTONE name/logo;
- copyright evidence and third-party license obligations.

Official portal: https://www.saip.gov.sa/

## Release decision
Owner approval is necessary but not sufficient: require security, science, IP and deployment gates. Patent screening must precede publication of potentially enabling technical detail. Do not claim any patent application has been filed or any patent granted.
