# LASERSTONE™ preview — review and feedback

This repository is under active development. The public preview is informational and does not provide live simulations or researcher accounts.

## Useful feedback
- Interface accessibility, mobile layout and language quality
- Clarity of research descriptions and scientific caveats
- Reproducibility and provenance requirements
- Feature suggestions that do not disclose confidential inventions

## Please do not submit publicly
Passwords, access tokens, private datasets, unpublished manuscripts, identifiable research participants, security exploits, or proprietary algorithms.

Feedback and feature ideas are not automatically accepted into the product, and do not transfer intellectual-property rights. Contributions requiring code or joint invention will need a separate agreement.

The owner will designate an approved feedback contact or moderated form before enabling public submissions. Until then, no feedback form or private login is active.
