# LS-PRIME-01 — Peer Review & Integrated Manuscript Evaluation

Status: approved design; implementation pending. Preserve current G24 acceptance scope; no release authorization.

## Workflows
- External peer review: local intake, journal policy and confidentiality gate, scientific assessment, numbered reviewer comments, separate confidential editor report, human recommendation.
- Author revision: one authoritative DOCX, verbatim numbered editor/reviewer comments, response, evidence, manuscript location, verification and disposition for each item.
- Editorial administration: correspondence, submission requirements, fee requests, proofs and publication tracking; human approval before sending.

## Input/output in existing leadership console
Inputs: manuscript, review comments, editor letter, journal requirements, authorized data and references. Outputs: reviewer report, revised master DOCX, point-by-point response, editor letter and QA report. Reuse council, evidence, physics, uncertainty and confidentiality engines. No duplicate launcher.

## Acceptance gates
Novelty and significance; physics and equations; methods; statistics and uncertainty; results consistency; reference/DOI verification; reproducibility; ethics and similarity; journal formatting. No fabricated findings or citations. No external processing of confidential peer-review material without journal permission and explicit authorization. No automated submission or recommendation without human approval.

## Document integrity
Preserve original IMRAD, styles, layout, editable equations, figures, tables and references. Mark author revisions green. Preserve original input unchanged and maintain one authoritative revised master. Validate numbering, cross-references and rendered layout. Track unresolved items explicitly; no claim of similarity clearance without actual authorized test.

## Implementation order
1. Local intake and case registry with confidentiality classification.
2. Point-by-point ledger and report generation.
3. DOCX preservation and visual acceptance.
4. Existing council console input/output integration.
5. Synthetic regression and authorized local operational acceptance.

No real manuscript data in repository or CI. External AI and similarity services remain disabled until authorized integration is verified.
